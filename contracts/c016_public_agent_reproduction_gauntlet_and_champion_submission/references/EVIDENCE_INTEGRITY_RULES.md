# Evidence integrity rules

1. Derive summaries from raw artifacts; never hand-copy headline numbers without validation.
2. Candidate identity must travel with each asynchronous result.
3. A zero denominator is `NOT_OBSERVED`, not 0%, 100%, PASS, or FAIL.
4. Keep screening, selection, and untouched confirmation seed sets separate.
5. Freeze ranking and thresholds before candidate results are known.
6. Do not select on the same untouched panel and then describe it as untouched validation.
7. Preserve both seat-specific and aggregate rates.
8. Label public score claims by evidence quality.
9. Package hash, source hash, deck hash, and uploaded hash must form one traceable chain.
10. `PASS` requires operational, evidence-integrity, competitive, package, permission, and accepted-reference gates.
11. A legal package can still be strategically weak.
12. An accepted Kaggle upload can still be an overall `PARTIAL` result.
