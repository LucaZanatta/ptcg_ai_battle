# License and Reuse Policy

- Record URL, retrieval time, SHA-256, archive inventory, and license evidence for every external artifact.
- Do not place externally licensed source in a competition package unless the license and competition rules permit it.
- Prefer clean-room reimplementation when reuse rights are unclear.
- Preserve algorithmic behavior through source mapping and conformance fixtures rather than copying code blindly.
- ByteRL paper equations and disclosed hyperparameters may be reimplemented; do not claim source-code identity when no complete source exists.
- Every package must contain only code and assets permitted for submission.
